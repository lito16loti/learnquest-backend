Our Purpose
Built to make learning actually engaging
GAMIFY is a web-based gamified e-learning system designed for Senior High School students at ICCT Colleges. We tackle low engagement in online and blended learning head-on — by turning every lesson into a game worth finishing.
Students earn XP for completing lessons and quizzes, unlock badges for hitting milestones, and level up as they master each subject. No boring walls of text — just progress you can see.
